        Multimodal Sensory E-skin and Synchronized Haptic Interfaces for Augmented Human-Robot Interaction

        I. Sim†, B. Bae†, S. Hasan, Y. Baek, D. H. Seo, W. Cho, J. Liu, Y. M. Song, J. Heo, T. Iqbal, S. Heo, K. Lee

        Nature Electronics (accepted)

        summary

        A flexible multimodal e-skin that combines near-infrared proximity sensing with piezoresistive tactile arrays on robotic fingertips. The sensors are synchronized with haptic interfaces, which gives real-time proximity mapping and tactile feedback for teleoperation and shared autonomy.
