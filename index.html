Inbo Sim

Ph.D. Candidate, Electrical & Computer Engineering · University of Virginia

I build multimodal electronic skin (e-skin) that lets robots feel and respond to the physical world. My work spans flexible electronics, heterogeneous chip integration, and human-robot interaction, from device fabrication up to on-body edge computing. I am advised by Prof. Kyusang Lee in the Intelligent Sensing Lab.
